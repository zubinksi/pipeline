How to read this

How far along

Early safety tests
First tests in people, mainly for safety.
Mid-stage testing
Does it work, and at what dose?
Final-stage trials
Large trials, often testing whether it prevents heart attacks. The last step before applying for approval.
FDA reviewing
The company has applied for approval.
Approved
Doctors can prescribe it.

Many drugs stop along the way. Even final-stage drugs are usually a few years from pharmacies.

Watching for

A short, hand-picked list of results that would change the picture. Dates are when a trial is expected to finish collecting data (they often slip); results usually follow within a few months.

LDL

The "bad" cholesterol that statins lower. Every drug here lowers LDL without being a statin or a daily pill. The percentages are how much LDL fell in trials, on top of whatever people were already taking.

Heart attacks

Lowering LDL isn't automatically the same as preventing heart attacks. "Prevents heart attacks" means a large trial showed fewer heart attacks and strokes; the others haven't been tested that way yet.
