About the ranges

The shaded band on each chart is a commonly used reference range for adults, not a personal target. Your own goals depend on your overall risk, so they're a conversation with your clinician.

LDL below 100 ("optimal"), HDL 40 or above (50 for women) and triglycerides below 150 mg/dL are the bands from the U.S. NCEP ATP III guideline, since replaced by risk-based guidelines that don't use fixed targets. Lp(a) below 75 nmol/L is the low range in the European Atherosclerosis Society's 2022 statement. hs-CRP below 1 mg/L is the lower-risk group in the AHA/CDC statement. ApoB has no single agreed cutoff, so no band is shown.
