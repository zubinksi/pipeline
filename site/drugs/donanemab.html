How it works

Clearing amyloid

  1. 01 Amyloid protein builds up into plaques between brain cells.
  2. 02 The plaques are linked to the damage behind memory loss.
  3. The drug The antibody sticks to amyloid and flags it.
  4. 04 The immune system clears it, slowing decline (not reversing it).
Notes

Clinical effectSlowed decline in memory and daily function in a large trial. About 37% of patients had brain swelling or bleeding on scans (ARIA), usually without symptoms. Source →

StatusFDA-approved in July 2024 for early symptomatic Alzheimer's. Source →
