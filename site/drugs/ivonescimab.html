One antibody that does two jobs: unleashes the immune system (PD-1) and starves tumors of blood supply (VEGF).

How it works

Two jobs in one antibody

  1. 01 Tumors switch off immune cells through a "brake" called PD-1.
  2. 02 They also grow new blood vessels using a signal called VEGF.
  3. The drug One antibody blocks both PD-1 and VEGF.
  4. 04 Immune cells can attack, and the tumor's blood supply is cut back.
