How it works

Clearing amyloid

  1. 01 Amyloid protein builds up into plaques between brain cells.
  2. 02 The plaques are linked to the damage behind memory loss.
  3. The drug The antibody sticks to amyloid and flags it.
  4. 04 The immune system clears it, slowing decline (not reversing it).
