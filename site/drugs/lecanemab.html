How it works

Clearing amyloid

  1. 01 Amyloid protein builds up into plaques between brain cells.
  2. 02 The plaques are linked to the damage behind memory loss.
  3. The drug The antibody sticks to amyloid and flags it.
  4. 04 The immune system clears it, slowing decline (not reversing it).
Notes

Clinical effectSlowed decline in memory and daily function in an 1,800-person trial (Clarity AD). Brain swelling or bleeding (ARIA) is a known risk. Source →

StatusApproved. The at-home weekly shot was approved for maintenance in 2025 and for starting treatment in July 2026. Source →
