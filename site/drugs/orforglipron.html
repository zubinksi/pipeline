FoundayoorforglipronEli Lilly

Lilly's GLP-1 pill. Unlike other GLP-1 pills it doesn't need to be taken on an empty stomach, and it's easier to make in bulk than shots.

How it works

Acting on gut hormones

  1. 01 After a meal, the gut releases hormones such as GLP-1.
  2. 02 They tell the brain you're full and slow the stomach.
  3. The drug The drug acts on these hormone signals (GLP-1, and for some also GIP or glucagon), and lasts far longer.
  4. 04 Appetite drops, people eat less, and weight falls steadily.
