ZepboundtirzepatideEli Lilly

A weekly shot that acts on two gut hormones (GIP and GLP-1) to reduce appetite. Sold as Zepbound for weight and Mounjaro for diabetes.

Approved
Weight loss
↓ 21%

CV outcomes

How it works

Acting on gut hormones

  1. 01 After a meal, the gut releases hormones such as GLP-1.
  2. 02 They tell the brain you're full and slow the stomach.
  3. The drug The drug acts on these hormone signals (GLP-1, and for some also GIP or glucagon), and lasts far longer.
  4. 04 Appetite drops, people eat less, and weight falls steadily.
Notes

CV outcomesA 15,000-person heart-outcome trial in people with obesity (SURMOUNT-MMO) is running, with results expected in 2027. In people with diabetes it matched an older drug with proven heart benefit (SURPASS-CVOT, 2025). Source →

StatusApproved and widely used. The best-selling drug in the world in 2026. Source →
