How it works · Removing the estrogen receptor
  1. 01 This breast cancer grows on estrogen signals, through its estrogen receptor.
  2. 02 A change in the ESR1 gene can make older hormone pills stop working.
  3. The drug The pill latches onto the receptor so the cell destroys it.
  4. 04 Without the receptor, the growth signal is cut off.
