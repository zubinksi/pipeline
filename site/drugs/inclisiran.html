How it works

Silencing the PCSK9 message

  1. 01 Liver cells copy the PCSK9 gene into a messenger (RNA) to make PCSK9.
  2. 02 PCSK9 marks the liver's LDL receptors to be destroyed.
  3. The drug The drug destroys the messenger, so far less PCSK9 is made, for months.
  4. 04 More receptors survive, so more LDL is cleared from the blood.
