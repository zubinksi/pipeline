How it works

Blocking PCSK9

  1. 01 Your liver pulls LDL out of the blood using receptors on its surface.
  2. 02 A protein called PCSK9 marks those receptors to be destroyed.
  3. The drug The drug grabs PCSK9 in the blood, so it can't.
  4. 04 More receptors survive, so more LDL is cleared from the blood.
