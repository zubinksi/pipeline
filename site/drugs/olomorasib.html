How it works · Turning off a stuck growth switch
  1. 01 KRAS is a switch that tells cells to grow.
  2. 02 A G12C change in the gene leaves the switch stuck on, driving the cancer.
  3. The drug The pill locks the faulty switch in its off position.
  4. 04 Taken with immunotherapy, the immune system attacks the tumor too.
