How it works

Silencing the Lp(a) message

  1. 01 Liver cells copy the LPA gene into a messenger (RNA) to make apo(a).
  2. 02 Apo(a) joins an LDL-like particle to form Lp(a).
  3. The drug The drug destroys the messenger, so far less apo(a) is made.
  4. 04 Fewer Lp(a) particles circulate in the blood.
