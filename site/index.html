How to read this

How far along

Early safety tests
First tests in people, mainly for safety.
Mid-stage testing
Does it work, and at what dose?
Final-stage trials
Large trials, often testing whether it prevents heart attacks. The last step before applying for approval.
FDA reviewing
The company has applied for approval.
Approved
Doctors can prescribe it.

Many drugs stop along the way. Even final-stage drugs are usually a few years from pharmacies.

What they lower

LDL
The "bad" cholesterol that statins lower.
Lp(a)
A cholesterol particle set mostly by your genes. High levels raise heart risk, and statins don't lower it. A one-time blood test measures it.
Triglycerides
Another blood fat linked to heart risk.

Everything listed here is a non-statin drug you wouldn't take daily. This is a news tracker, not medical advice.
